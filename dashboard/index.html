How this radar works

Implied value = TVL × Mcat × Fgrowth × Fage × Fchain × Plaunch, for a protocol that records no token. One whose token already trades is valued at its observed market cap, and marked as a trading anchor.

  • Mcat — the comps multiple: the fitted size curve, log(market cap) = 6.96 + 0.52·log(TVL), least squares on the 128 trading protocols, so the multiple falls as TVL grows. Top comps: Dexs ×0.34 (9), Yield ×0.19 (9), Lending ×0.15 (8), Basis Trading ×0.65 (7), RWA ×0.20 (7).
  • Fgrowth — 7-day TVL momentum, capped at −50% to +100%.
  • Fage — a hump: 1.15 at two years without a token, fading to 0.95; unknown age 0.90.
  • Fchain — 1.00 for one chain, 1.05 for two or three, 1.10 for four or more.
  • Plaunch — a stated prior, not an observation: 0.65 watchlist-eligible, 0.50 other pre-token.

Leave-one-out. On the 128 trading tokens (multiple only, each predicted with itself removed): the curve's median miss is ×3.40 (32% within 2×, 42% within 3×), vs ×5.07 for the global median and ×4.86 for the category median. Internal consistency, not validation against launches.

Walk-forward. Fit on the older 50%, 60% and 70% of listed tokens, test on the newer rest (curve vs global median): ×3.01/×2.88/×3.41 vs ×5.29/×5.25/×5.29 (n=48, 38, 29). The curve beats the flat median on newer tokens at every cutoff.

Stepwise. Five ways to predict a trading token's market cap, scored by leave-one-out (median miss, share within 3×, Spearman): M0 global median ×5.07, 35%, 0.42; M1 category median ×4.86, 30%, 0.38; M2 size curve (OLS) ×3.40, 42%, 0.40; M3 curve × growth × age × chain ×3.43, 43%, 0.39; M4 size curve (Huber) ×3.51, 42%, 0.41.

The curve wins on median miss; the extra factors in M3 do not improve it, and the robust Huber fit (M4, slope 0.55 vs OLS 0.52, median miss ×3.51) does not beat plain least squares either. The flat median ranks slightly better (Spearman 0.42 vs 0.40) but misses further on size — the curve is kept because it prices size, not because it wins every column.

Stability. The trading pool is split into five folds by slug order, the curve refit on each four-fifths; the top 20 pre-token rows keep 20/20/20/20/20 of their members under every refit (median overlap 20, worst 20). The ranking's head does not depend on which fifth is held out. Each row's rank range under those refits (plus the Huber fit) is shown under its rank.

Negative results. A second size term (log TVL squared) was fit and scored by leave-one-out: median miss ×3.38 vs ×3.40 for the straight line — a gain of 0.02, nothing meaningful, so the model keeps the straight line. A time-weighted dollar-days share (capital × days held, TVL grown at the observed 7-day rate) was also tested against the plain pro-rata share: the median net ratio is 1.00 and no row changes sign, so the plan keeps the simpler pro-rata share.

Farm economics. Every plan's net is the pro-rata reward at the farmer allocation (slider above, default 10%, 5–15%), minus gas, fees and the forgone yield (a stated 4% annual benchmark over the hold). “Pays after N days” assumes the reward accrues linearly over the hold; costs accrue $2 a week plus the forgone yield daily. Verdict: Robust when the low-end net (implied value divided by the curve's median miss) is positive at that deposit, Thin when only the implied-value net is, Skip otherwise — move the deposit toggle or the slider to see it move. Farming all 40 watchlist protocols at the plan capitals costs $4,100 of capital and $1,335 of fees and forgone yield for about $30 of expected reward — the book loses about $1,305 even if all 40 launch at their implied values, so no book-level break-even exists at these sizes.

Confidence grade. Each row scores /100: 40 base + 15 watchlist + up to 10 age (10 at ≥1 year, 5 at 180–364 days) + 10 positive seven-day growth + up to 10 size (10 at TVL ≥$100M, 5 at $50–100M) + 15 when the low-end $10,000 net is positive, or 15 for a trading anchor (value observed, not modelled). A ≥80, B ≥60, else C. On this board: 8 A · 122 B · 130 C.

Early finds. A pre-token protocol, not on the watchlist, that is young (under 180 days) or moving fast (seven-day TVL above +20%): 48 rows on this board. They are the launch candidates the watchlist has not caught yet — and the ones with the least history, so read their grades with the rest of the row.

Known limits. P_launch is a stated prior, never calibrated against launches. Implied values are a relative ranking signal, not a price target.

The board holds 260 rows: the whole 40-protocol watchlist, the 36 highest-valued trading anchors outside bridges, staked products and exchanges, every pre-token protocol outside those categories (174), and the 10 trading-anomaly protocols outside them — 214 pre-token rows in all. The 498 left off are counted by reason: 258 record a symbol but no observable value, 172 sit in an excluded category, 68 are trading protocols below the anchor cut. Each row carries a farm plan with concrete costs and times, computed in-page from the board data.
