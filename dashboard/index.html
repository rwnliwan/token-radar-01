How this radar works

Implied value = TVL × Mcat × Fgrowth × Fage × Fchain × Plaunch, for a protocol that records no token. A protocol whose token already trades is valued at its observed market cap instead, and marked as a trading anchor.

  • Mcat — the comps multiple: the median market-cap/TVL of trading protocols in the same category and of similar size (TVL within 0.2×–5×), winsorized to 0.05–20, at least 5 comps, else the category median, else the global median (0.205). No name is ever special-cased.
  • Fgrowth — seven-day TVL momentum, capped at −50% to +100%.
  • Fage — a hump: trust grows to 1.15 at two years without a token, then fades to 0.95; unknown age scores 0.90.
  • Fchain — 1.00 for one chain, 1.05 for two or three, 1.10 for four or more.
  • Plaunch — a stated prior, not an observation: 0.65 for watchlist-eligible protocols, 0.50 for other pre-token ones.

Pre-token means all three token fields are null (market cap, CoinGecko id, symbol). The board holds 200 rows: the whole 40-protocol watchlist plus the 160 highest-valued others outside bridges, staked products and exchanges. Each row carries a farm plan with concrete costs and times, computed in-page from the board data.
